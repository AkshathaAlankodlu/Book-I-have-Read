One True Loves

Author: Taylor Jenkins Reid

Genre: Fiction,Drama

Rating: 4.3

Blurb: It has been 3 years since Emma's husband Jesse has got missing and being declared dead. Emma receives a call from an unknown number one evening when she was out with her parents and fiance celebarting her father's 64th birthday,only to find it was none other than Jesse himself.

Review: Thoughout the story I was curious to know on whom Emma would choose to live with. I was happy and convinced when she finally chooses one among her husband and fiance. It was good to see how understanding they were in making that decision with mutual consent.

That's not my name

Author: Megan Lally

Genre: Fiction,Thriller

Rating: 4.3

Blurb: A teenage girl wakes up in a ditch with no memory of who she is. When a man arrives at the police station claiming to be her father, she wants to believe him, but something feels off. While she is trying to piece together her past ,Drew is desperately searching for his girlfriend Lola who was with him before disappearing mysteriously. So the whole town believes it was Drew who killed Lola.

Review: I was clueless and confused on what was happening initially. Later as the story builds everything started making sense. It is after Drew leaves his town searching for Lola himself that the story becomes more interesting. Beyond the thrill and suspense, the story becomes emotional and heart piercing.

Almond

Author: Sohn Won-pyung

Genre: Fiction,Drama

Rating: 4

Blurb: Yunjae, is born with a brain condition called alexithymia, which makes it impossible for him to feel or identify emotions like fear, anger, or love. After a family tragedy, he must learn to live on his own. When he meets Gon, a boy full of anger, their friendship helps Yunjae discover what it means to care, connect, and grow.The book shows on what it is to be different from others.

Review: It was an easy read and different from the usual genres I pick up. It felt more like a conceptual drama, which kept me curious throughout. I was especially eager to see how Yunjae would cope with his condition, and the story delivered that journey in a thoughtful way. Overall, it was a good read with what felt like a hopeful, happy ending.

We live here now

Author: Sarah Pinborough

Genre: Fiction,Thriller

Rating: 4.2

Blurb: Emily and Freddie move into a new house after a difficult time. Freddie is hopeful, but Emily isn’t happy with the decision. She tries to go along with it for his sake,until strange things start happening in the house.

Review: It was a decent read, though I was expecting a different twist in the story. Honestly, the chances of something like this happening in real life feel pretty slim, so it works better as pure fiction. Still, it was enjoyable, and the ending came across as a happy one 😄.

Who will cry when you die

Author: Robin Sharma

Genre: Non-Fiction

Rating: 4

Review: I’m usually not into non‑fiction, but this book convinced me to give the genre more attention. The author shares life tips in short, easy‑to‑read chapters, which made it feel very approachable. I couldn’t apply everything right away, but even the few changes I tried had a big impact on me. Overall, it was a refreshing and worthwhile read.

Author: Bhaskar Chattopadhyay

Genre: Fiction,Crime thriller

Rating: 4.3

Blurb: On a rainy night, a man discovers a body hanging upside down from one of the extended metallic pole of an antenna. A note left by the killer warns of three more murders. Detective Rathod catches the suspect and sends him to prison. But three months later, the killings start again, following the same pattern. Now Rathod must face a mystery that is far from over.

Review: The story is captivating and gripping from the beginning. The description of each scene made me feel like I was watching a crime thriller movie. Even though all the killings were reasonable, I felt the ending could have been better.

The Inmate

Author: Freida McFadden

Genre: Fiction, Thriller

Rating: 4.5

Blurb: Brooke starts working at a prison and comes face to face with someone from her past. As secrets unfold, she is drawn into a dangerous game of lies and shocking twists.

Review: Eventhough I couldn’t finish it in one sitting, at one point I stayed up until 2 a.m. just to know what happens next. Some scenes even made me emotional, and overall it was definitely a good read. This is the second book I’ve read by Freida, and it didn’t disappoint me. She’s becoming one of my go-to authors for thriller novels. The twist in the last few chapters, with a twist inside a twist, completely changed how I felt about the story and made me rethink the emotions I had earlier.

When I kill you

Author: B A Paris

Genre: Fiction,Thriller

Rating: 4.4

Blurb: Fourteen years ago, Elle Nugent watched a young student get into a stranger’s car. When the girl was found dead the next day, Elle became dangerously obsessed with proving the driver was the killer, which eventually ruined her life and forced her to start over as Nell.Now after many years, Nell is haunted by the feeling she is being followed and fears that someone from her past haa finally found her

Review: I kept guessing who the killer was, but the book never leaked its suspense, making it a truly satisfying read. Would love to read more books by BA Paris

My Husband's Wife

Author: Alice Feeney

Genre: Fiction,Thriller

Rating: 4.8

Blurb: Eden comes home from her evening walk to find another woman in her house, claiming to be her husband’s wife. To her shock, even her husband denies knowing her. Meanwhile, Birdy has been living with cancer for six months and is counting down her final days. As their lives connect, hidden truths come to light with expectable twists until the end.

Review: At first, Eden’s point of view in the opening chapter reminded me of Dark Matter. This book turned out to be a thrilling read with plenty of “woaah” moments, especially towards the end. The twists kept coming up until the final chapter, answering every question along the way. I finished it in just 4-5 sittings. Now I understand why people call Alice Feeney the queen of twists and I completely agree with it. This was my first book by her, and I’m excited to read more books by her.

The Exception to the Rule

Author: Christina Lauren

Genre: Fiction, Romance

Rating: 4.4

Blurb: A wrong Valentine’s Day email brings two strangers together. What starts as a yearly exchange of messages slowly turns into something more.

Review: I just happened to look at my read list and realised I had been reading only thrillers after "one true loves" ,until now . I had actually forgotten how it feels to read a good rom-com.This book being hardly 98 pages brightened my mood instantly.It was a short and sweet read. I really think I should go back to my tradition of reading rom-com and thrillers alternatively.
